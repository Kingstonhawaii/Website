# Website
What is this code?
It's a basic webpage for a clothing brand called "HiddenWorld." It shows the logo, menu links, a big header, some info about the brand, and some products you can look at.

How does the webpage look?
Header (top part): Has the logo, menu links like Home, About, Mens, Womens, Contact, and icons for search, cart, and profile.
Hero section: Big title with "HiddenWorld" and a subtitle "Only Best For You!"
Main content:
About Us: Tells about the brand.
Best Selling Items: Shows some clothes you can click on to see more details.
Footer (bottom): Has icons for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and a copyright note.
How do the products work?
There are 3 products shown.
When you click on a product or the "View Product" button, a pop-up appears with more info.
The pop-up shows the product name, picture, description, and options to choose size.
There are buttons to add or remove the item from your shopping bag (they just show alerts).
How does the pop-up (modal) work?
It is hidden by default.
When you click on a product, JavaScript makes it appear.
You can close it by clicking the "X" button.
What do the scripts do?
showProduct: Displays the product details in the pop-up.
closeProduct: Hides the pop-up.
addToBag / removeFromBag: Show alert messages when you click them.
navigateTo: To handle menu link clicks (in this code, it just shows an alert).
